# Create Your First Proof

Welcome to Proof of Existence. This guide walks you through the full flow — from login to submission — with no crypto knowledge required.

> Sign in with Google → enter the canvas → draw for 10+ seconds → click "Submit Proof" → choose Standard Proof (free). Done. Your trail gets recorded on the Polygon blockchain at UTC 00:00 each day.

---

## What Do You Need?

Just a web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge). No wallet, no upfront funds, no technical setup.

---

## Step 1: Log In

Visit [proofexistence.com](/) and click "Login / Connect Wallet" in the top right corner.

### Social Login (Recommended for beginners)

Sign in with an existing account: Google, Facebook, Apple, Line, or X (Twitter).

**Web3Auth automatically creates a secure blockchain wallet for you.** You don't need to understand how that works or save any private keys — it's just like logging into a regular app.

### External Wallet

If you already have MetaMask or another Web3 wallet, you can connect it directly instead.

---

## Step 2: Enter the Canvas

Click the [canvas](/canvas) to enter the 3D space. You'll see a dark, infinite environment — this is the shared Global Canvas where your trail will live permanently.

---

## Step 3: Draw Your Light Trail

- **Click and hold** to start drawing
- **Move your mouse** (keep holding) to extend the trail
- **Release** to pause
- Draw for at least **10 seconds** to create a valid proof

Don't overthink it. Your first trail doesn't need to mean anything in particular — it's a timestamp of your movement at this moment, and that's enough.

---

## Step 4: Submit Your Proof

Once you've drawn for at least 10 seconds, click "Submit Proof" and choose a proof type.

### Standard Proof (Free)

Your trail gets batched with everyone else's that day into a Merkle Tree, then submitted to the Polygon blockchain at UTC 00:00. The protocol covers all gas fees. You also earn Time26 tokens as a record of participation.

| Feature      | Details                         |
| ------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Cost         | Free                            |
| Verification | Daily batch at UTC 00:00        |
| Technology   | Merkle tree aggregation         |
| On-chain     | Yes, via daily batch settlement |
| Token reward | Time26                          |

### Perpetual Proof (Paid)

Mints an NFT immediately and writes your data to Arweave (decentralized permanent storage) in real time. Good for collectors who want instant, tradeable ownership.

| Feature      | Details                                 |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Cost         | POL or TIME26 tokens                    |
| Verification | Instant (within seconds)                |
| NFT          | ERC-721, minted to your wallet          |
| Storage      | Arweave permanent decentralized storage |
| Badge        | Exclusive collector badge               |

Pricing: base fee + per-second rate for trails over 45 seconds (first 45 seconds included in the base fee).

Not sure which to pick? Start with the free Standard Proof.

## FAQ

**Is it safe to log in with my social account?**

Web3Auth uses MPC (multi-party computation) to create your wallet — it never stores your private key, and it doesn't access your social account's content. The login flow goes through official OAuth only.

**What's the difference between Standard and Perpetual Proof storage?**

Standard Proof data is temporarily held on the protocol's servers, then uploaded to Arweave in a daily batch. Perpetual Proof data is written to Arweave immediately at the time of minting — it doesn't depend on our servers. Both end up on-chain, but Perpetual is faster and fully independent.

**What happens if I draw for less than 10 seconds?**

The submit button won't activate until you've reached 10 seconds. This minimum is enforced at the contract level, not just in the UI.

**What is Time26?**

POE's native token. You earn it through Existence Mining (Standard Proof participation). It can also be used to pay for Perpetual Proof at a 20% discount vs. USD. See the [whitepaper](/whitepaper) for details.

**Can I use this on mobile?**

Yes. Desktop gives you the best drawing experience, but the canvas works on mobile touchscreen too.
